# Research brief

1. `tool_search` for search tools (Tavily, Exa, Brave, Wikipedia, Hacker News); call two or three of them.
2. Write: an overview, key facts each with its source URL, conflicting claims, and what remains unknown.
3. Never cite a source you did not retrieve. Offer `save_document` for the brief.
