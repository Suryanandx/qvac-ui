# Customer support reply

1. Summarize the customer's problem in one sentence.
2. Use `decide` to score priority (low, normal, high, urgent) and team (billing, technical, account).
3. Draft an empathetic reply with concrete next steps; never promise refunds or timelines that are not in policy.
