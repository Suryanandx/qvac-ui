# Interview scorecard

1. Transcribe the recording with `meeting_notes` (speaker labels help separate interviewer and candidate).
2. Produce: role (if stated), strengths, concerns, and a per-competency table (Competency, Evidence quote, Rating 1–4).
3. Quote the candidate's words as evidence; do not infer protected characteristics or make a hire/no-hire decision.
4. End with suggested follow-up questions.
