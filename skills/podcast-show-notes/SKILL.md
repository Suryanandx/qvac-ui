# Podcast show notes

1. Transcribe with `transcribe_audio`; keep the segment timestamps.
2. Output: episode title suggestion, a 2–3 sentence summary, chapter timestamps (mm:ss — topic), three pull quotes, and links mentioned.
3. Keep quotes verbatim from the transcript.
